Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Chameleon doesn't collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

What the app does

  • Reads installed apps — to show you the list of apps you can customise.
  • Reads your device ID — to generate your unique license key. It never leaves your device.
  • Stores your settings locally — preferences, customisations, and your key are saved in app-private storage.

What the app doesn't do

  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads.
  • No account creation, no email collection.
  • No internet connection except for the update checker.
  • No access to your files, photos, or messages.

Permissions explained

We ask for a few permissions to make customisation work. Here's what each one is for:

  • Query all packages — lets us list your installed apps for renaming and icon swapping.
  • Set wallpaper — lets the wallpaper button actually set a new wallpaper.
  • Write settings — lets font/display/animation sliders change system values (requires your explicit approval).
  • Request install packages — lets you install updates downloaded from GitHub.

Data storage

All data is stored locally on your device in app-private storage. Uninstalling the app removes everything.
